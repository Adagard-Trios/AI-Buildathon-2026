# What it looks like

"Guaranteed 30% monthly returns", "double your money in 60 days", "zero risk". Real investments
cannot promise a fixed return, and no legitimate fund guarantees anything near this.

# Why it works

A precise number sounds calculated, and "guaranteed" removes the fear of loss. Early payouts are
often paid from later victims' deposits, which makes the promise look true for a while.

# What to do

Do not pay. Investment scams are the most commonly reported type in Southeast Asia.[^gasa-2025]
